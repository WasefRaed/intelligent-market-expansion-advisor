# Source provenance and research leads

## Reviewed authoritative references (2026-10-09)

The machine-readable registry `data/source_registry.json` is the **single source of truth** for the curated official references. Each record contains a precise supported claim, public URL, review date (at registry level), and limitations. See `docs/PHASE5_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE.md` for how evidence is selected and presented.

Reviewed categories:
- Federal Tax Authority: VAT mandatory registration, Corporate Tax rate bands on **taxable income**, and Corporate Tax registration service.
- Dubai Municipality: food-establishment requirements and Food Code materials.
- UAE Government Portal: food-safety responsibility in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.
- Visit Dubai: JLT neighborhood description (mix of offices, homes, restaurants, public transport).
- Experience Abu Dhabi: Al Zahiyah neighborhood description (established central neighborhood).

The catalog is **frozen offline evidence**; it is not fresh web retrieval on every query and is not a substitute for official advice or current page review at investment time.

## Unverified planning data and leads

The `data/market_snapshot.json` includes internal strategic heuristics, competitor candidates, menu-price hypotheses, and external links collected during project development. Those are **research leads, not validated current business facts**.

Do not treat any internal location rank, price band, delivery fee, rental description, or presumed demand level as an observed value. Previous statements about specific competitor menu prices are **not** verified by the 2026-10-09 source registry and should not be described as current quotations.

## Business validation tasks before signing a lease

Collect formal site-specific offers, technical site assessments, licensing feedback from relevant authorities, up-to-date competitor menus, delivery-platform commercial agreements, food-supplier quotes, and customer/footfall observations. Convert the qualitative comparison into a documented location-specific decision once those inputs are present.
